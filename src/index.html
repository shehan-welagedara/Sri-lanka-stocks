Features

Everything a restaurant runs on, in one place

A point of sale your staff learn in minutes

Take orders at the counter or the table, send them to the kitchen, and settle the bill the way the guest wants to pay.

  • Dine-in, takeaway and delivery
  • Kitchen and bar order tickets
  • Split bills across cash, card and QR payments
  • Allergy alerts on every dish
  • Manager approval for voids and discounts

Your day, on your phone

Open the app wherever you are and see how today compares with the same day last week, with anything that needs your attention.

  • Net sales, covers and average spend
  • Month to date against your target
  • Approvals and tasks waiting for you
  • End-of-day report sent to managers

Stock and food cost that keep themselves up to date

Every dish has a recipe, so every sale reduces the right ingredients. You see what you have, what it is worth and what to reorder.

  • Recipes with portion cost and food cost percentage
  • Purchase orders and goods received
  • Stock counts, waste and transfers
  • Stock value at any time

Reports and accounts without re-typing

Sales flow into your reports and your books automatically, so month end is a review and not a data-entry job.

  • Daily revenue, item sales and payment types
  • Best and worst selling dishes
  • Supplier bills, payments and bank reconciliation
  • Profit and loss and balance sheet
  • Audit trail of every change

Roles and PINs

Each employee signs in with their own PIN and sees only what their role allows.
