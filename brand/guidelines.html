Brand guidelines

How Enkira looks, sounds and names its products. Use this for the software, the website, adverts, proposals and printed material.

01 · Brand idea

Master every part of your business

Enkira takes its name from Enki, the Sumerian god of wisdom, craft and fresh water, who knew every art and kept the measures of civilisation. Enkira gives a business owner the same command: one system that knows the counter, the kitchen, the stores and the books.

Name

Written Enkira, capital E only. Said en-KEE-ra. Never ENKIRA in running text, and never used as a verb.

Tagline

Master every part of your business.
Short form for adverts: One system. Complete control.

Descriptor

POS and back office for Sri Lankan restaurants. Use beside the logo wherever people may not know what Enkira is.

Promise

Know your sales, stock and profit every night.

Who it is for

Owners of restaurants, cafés and bakeries with one to three outlets.

Why believe it

Sri Lankan tax rules built in, stock deducted from every sale, and it runs on the phone in your pocket.

02 · Logo

The mark and the wordmark

The mark is three flowing lines that form the letter E. They stand for Enki's streams of water and for the three layers Enkira joins: selling, stock and accounts. The wordmark is set in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold and supplied as outlines.

Clear space and size

Keep a margin equal to half the mark's height on every side. Smallest sizes: lockup 96 px or 25 mm wide; mark 16 px or 5 mm.

Do not

  • Change the colours or the gradient direction.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows.
  • Retype the wordmark in another font.
  • Place the colour logo on a busy photo; use the white version.
  • Put the mark inside another shape.
  • Use the mark as the letter E in a word.

03 · Colour

Deep ink, lit by one gradient

Most surfaces are white or ink. The gradient is the signature and is kept for the logo, primary buttons and one highlight per screen. Text never sits on the gradient except white, bold, 16 px or larger.
